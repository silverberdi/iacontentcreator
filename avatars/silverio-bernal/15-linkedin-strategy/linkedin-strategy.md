# LinkedIn Strategic Role

## Purpose

LinkedIn is the primary:
- authority platform,
- professional distribution layer,
- networking environment.

---

## Content Focus

Primary themes:
- systems thinking,
- organizational friction,
- AI-native operations,
- operational architecture,
- enterprise transformation.

---

## Audience

Primary audience:
- senior engineers,
- architects,
- tech leads,
- technical managers,
- enterprise operators.

---

## Tone

LinkedIn content should feel:
- thoughtful,
- calm,
- technically mature,
- strategically relevant.

---

## Avoid

- shallow AI trends,
- engagement bait,
- generic productivity content,
- excessive self-promotion.
