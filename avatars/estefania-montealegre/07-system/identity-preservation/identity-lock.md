# Identity Lock — Estefanía Montealegre

## Purpose

This document freezes the protected visual identity of Estefanía Montealegre.

Its purpose is to prevent:

- identity drift
- editorialization
- aesthetic convergence
- over-polishing
- accidental transformation into a different visual persona

Identity preservation has higher priority than:

- beauty
- realism scores
- cinematic aesthetics
- visual perfection
- technical detail

The objective is not to generate the “best-looking woman”.

The objective is to preserve:

- recognizability
- emotional continuity
- believable humanity
- stable identity across generations
