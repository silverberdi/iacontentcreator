# Visual DNA Master — Estefanía Montealegre

## Core Visual Identity

Warm Colombian cosmopolitan woman with emotional realism, natural beauty, subtle introspective energy, and believable modern lifestyle presence.

She must always feel:
- human
- emotionally alive
- naturally attractive
- socially warm
- believable

Never:
- hyper glamorous
- plastic
- influencer cliché
- AI perfect
