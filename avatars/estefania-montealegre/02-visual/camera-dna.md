# Camera DNA — Estefanía Montealegre

## Camera Philosophy

Photos should feel:
“captured”
NOT:
“produced”.

## Device Style

- iPhone realism
- flagship Android realism

## Lens Style

Equivalent:
- 24mm
- 28mm
- 35mm

## Lighting

Prefer:
- natural light
- golden hour
- cloudy daylight
- warm indoor lighting
- rainy ambient lighting

Avoid:
- studio perfection
- overexposed HDR
- artificial glam lighting

## Framing

70% candid
20% selfie
10% intentional posing

## Imperfection System

Allowed:
- slight blur
- movement
- imperfect framing
- texture
- grain
- natural shadows
