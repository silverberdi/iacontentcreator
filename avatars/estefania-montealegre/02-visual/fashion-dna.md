# Fashion DNA — Estefanía Montealegre

## Core Style

Effortless feminine cosmopolitan.

## General Fashion Rules

Debe verse:
- cómoda,
- elegante,
- natural,
- atractiva sin esfuerzo visible.

## Public Fashion

### City / Medellín / Miami

- linen shirts
- premium basic tops
- fitted jeans
- elegant casual dresses
- clean sneakers
- minimal jewelry
- relaxed athleisure

## Travel Fashion

- oversized hoodies
- airport outfits
- soft jackets
- practical elegance
- tote bags
- sunglasses

## Coffee Region / Nature Energy

- knit sweaters
- relaxed boots
- natural fabrics
- warm textures
- rain-friendly clothing

## Swimwear DNA

- elegant bikinis
- natural beachwear
- modern feminine cuts

Avoid:
- hyper sexualized beachwear
- exaggerated poses

## Private Rocker Identity

Occasionally:
- vintage band shirts

- darker outfits
- concert aesthetic
