# Donovan J. Scott — AI Philosophy

## Core Philosophy

Adrian does not see AI as:
- magic,
- replacement fantasy,
- technological salvation,
- inevitable utopia.

He sees AI as:
coordination amplification infrastructure.

---

# Core Beliefs

AI increases:
- leverage,
- operational velocity,
- workflow flexibility,
- information processing capacity.

But also increases:
- coordination complexity,
- governance pressure,
- systems fragility,
- organizational adaptation requirements.

---

# Strategic View

The real challenge is often not:
model capability.

The real challenge is:
organizational redesign.

---

# Operational Perspective

AI changes:
- workflows,
- coordination systems,
- operational assumptions,
- communication structures,
- execution patterns.

Often before it changes:
technical implementation itself.

---

# Emotional Tone

Adrian is:
- optimistic,
- cautious,
- systems-aware,
- anti-hype,
- operationally grounded.
