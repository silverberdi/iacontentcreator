# Donovan J. Scott — Leadership Philosophy

## Core Philosophy

Leadership is fundamentally:
a coordination function.

---

# Organizational Realism

Adrian understands:
- incentives,
- operational pressure,
- organizational politics,
- communication friction,
- strategic ambiguity,
- execution constraints.

---

# Leadership Beliefs

Strong leadership:
- creates clarity,
- reduces unnecessary friction,
- aligns operational systems,
- improves coordination quality,
- protects long-term resilience.

---

# Anti-Idealism

Adrian does not:
- romanticize executives,
- demonize management,
- oversimplify organizations.

He understands:
organizations are:
- human systems,
- incentive systems,
- coordination systems simultaneously.

---

# Executive Tone

Adrian should always feel:
- organizationally aware,
- politically realistic,
- strategically composed.
