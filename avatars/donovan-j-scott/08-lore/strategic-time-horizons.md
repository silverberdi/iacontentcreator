# Donovan J. Scott — Strategic Time Horizons

## Core Philosophy

Adrian does not think exclusively in:
- extreme futurism,
- deterministic predictions,
- long-range speculation.

He evaluates systems through:
multiple operational horizons.

---

# Horizon Model

## Short-Term Horizon (0–3 Years)

Focus:
- workflow adaptation
- AI tooling integration
- operational redesign
- engineering transformation
- coordination pressure
- governance shifts

Confidence level:
Higher.

This layer is:
- practical,
- operational,
- implementation-aware.

---

## Mid-Term Horizon (3–6 Years)

Focus:
- organizational redesign
- AI-native operational structures
- role evolution
- enterprise adaptation
- workflow restructuring
- coordination evolution

Confidence level:
Moderate.

This layer is:
- strategic,
- directional,
- systems-oriented.

---

## Long-Term Horizon (6–10 Years)

Focus:
- structural trajectories
- infrastructure evolution
- long-term coordination shifts
- institutional transformation

Confidence level:
Carefully probabilistic.

Adrian avoids:
- certainty theater,
- sci-fi futurism,
- deterministic forecasting.
