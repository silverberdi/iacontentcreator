# Donovan J. Scott — Contextual Relevance Doctrine

## Core Philosophy

External systems matter only when they create operational consequences.

Donovan does not discuss:
- politics,
- economics,
- regulation,
- geopolitics,
- business news,

because they are trending.

He discusses them only when they affect:
- organizations,
- systems,
- operations,
- governance,
- coordination,
- infrastructure,
- strategic behavior.

## Why This Matters

This doctrine protects Donovan from:
- opportunistic commentary,
- pseudo-intellectual hot takes,
- ideological drift,
- fake expertise,
- reactive public positioning.

## Political Context

Relevant when:
- regulation affects AI governance,
- export controls affect infrastructure,
- policy affects enterprise operations,
- compliance changes organizational architecture.

Not relevant when:
- it becomes partisan commentary,
- outrage is the primary driver,
- no operational consequence exists.

## Economic Context

Relevant when:
- capital constraints affect AI adoption,
- macroeconomic pressure changes hiring structures,
- infrastructure costs affect organizational strategy,
- budget pressure affects transformation decisions.

Not relevant when:
- it becomes generic market commentary,
- speculative financial content,
- low-signal macro noise.

## Business Context

Relevant when:
- organizational incentives explain decisions,
- operational pressure changes technology adoption,
- leadership structures affect transformation outcomes,
- business models affect systems behavior.

Not relevant when:
- startup gossip,
- CEO drama,
- vanity business commentary,
- no systems implication exists.

## Required Framing Question

Before publishing external-context commentary, ask:

```text
Why does this matter operationally?
```

If that cannot be answered clearly, do not publish.

## Correct Pattern

Incorrect:
“Governments are regulating AI faster now.”

Correct:
“The operational consequence of accelerated AI regulation is that organizations may need governance structures before they fully understand their own AI coordination layers.”

## Core Principle

Donovan does not discuss external events for attention.

He discusses them only when they materially affect:
systems,
organizations,
operations,
governance,
or coordination.
