# Donovan J. Scott — Publishing Workflows

## Core Philosophy

Publishing is signal-driven, not calendar-driven.

Donovan publishes when:
- there is meaningful signal,
- there are operational implications,
- there is strategic relevance,
- the audience gains clarity.

## LinkedIn

Role:
Primary authority surface.

Objective:
- executive positioning,
- strategic legitimacy,
- professional trust,
- discoverability,
- C-level relevance.

Cadence:
2–4 posts per week.
Preferred: 3 high-signal posts per week.

## X

Role:
Systems notebook.

Objective:
- idea fragments,
- thought evolution,
- signal detection,
- intellectual experimentation,
- lightweight presence.

Cadence:
Flexible and restrained.

## YouTube

Role:
Deep synthesis layer.

Objective:
- narrative authority,
- long-form reasoning,
- intellectual depth,
- strategic storytelling.

Cadence:
1 per week ideal.
2 per month acceptable if quality demands it.

## Podcast

Role:
Strategic intimacy layer.

Cadence:
1–3 episodes per month.

Feeling:
Private strategic dialogue, not creator content machine.

## Executive Briefings

Role:
Premium authority layer.

Objective:
- enterprise relevance,
- monetizable insight,
- C-level positioning,
- strategic guidance.

## Publishing Workflow

```text
Research Intake
    ↓
Signal Detection
    ↓
Strategic Interpretation
    ↓
Platform Adaptation
    ↓
Voice Alignment
    ↓
Governance Review
    ↓
Human Approval
    ↓
Publishing
    ↓
Archive & Knowledge Reinforcement
```

## Archive Requirements

Every published asset should store:
- source,
- topic,
- intellectual territory,
- emotional tone,
- platform,
- related concepts,
- reuse opportunities,
- doctrine references,
- publication date,
- performance notes.

## Final Principle

Every platform changes format.

No platform changes Donovan’s doctrine.
