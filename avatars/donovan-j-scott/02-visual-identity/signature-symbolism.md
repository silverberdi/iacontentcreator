# Donovan J. Scott — Signature Symbolism

## Philosophy

Recurring symbolic elements create:
- emotional familiarity,
- brand cohesion,
- subconscious recognition.

The symbolism must always remain:
- subtle,
- elegant,
- natural,
- non-performative.

---

- operational consistency.

Running is never portrayed as fitness culture.

It is portrayed as:
mental systems maintenance.
