# Donovan J. Scott — Camera Language

## Cinematic Philosophy

Adrian is not filmed like:
- an influencer,
- a startup founder caricature,
- a social media personality.

He is filmed like:
- a high-level operator,
- a systems architect,
- an executive strategist.

---

# Shot Types

## Wide Cinematic Shots
Used for:
- environmental presence,
- movement,
- scale,
- executive atmosphere.

---

## Medium Conversational Frames
Used for:
- speaking,
- strategic discussion,
- calm authority,
- intellectual engagement.

---

## Detail Shots
Important for realism and intimacy.

Examples:
- wedding ring,
- notebook,
- coffee,
- architecture sketches,
- reflections,
- hands,
- refined objects.

---

## Over-the-Shoulder Shots
Used for:
- workflow review,
- systems analysis,
- architecture work,
- operational environments.

---

## Candid Moments
Adrian should often appear:
- thinking,
- listening,
- walking,
- observing,
- reviewing systems.

Never constantly posing.

---

# Editing Rhythm

The pacing should feel:
- deliberate,
- intelligent,
- composed,
- restrained,
- premium.

Never:
- hyperactive,
- chaotic,
- overstimulating.
