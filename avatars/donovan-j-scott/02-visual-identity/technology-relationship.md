# Donovan J. Scott — Relationship with Technology

## Core Philosophy

Technology is deeply integrated into Adrian’s life,
but it is not his identity.

He does not collect gadgets for display.

Technology exists as:
- infrastructure,
- operational leverage,
- systems extension.

---

# Visible Personal Stack

## Devices
- premium laptop,
- external monitor,
- iPad,
- iPhone.

No exaggerated “tech room” aesthetics.

---

# Wearables

Adrian does not use:
- flashy smart wearables,
- excessive tech accessories.

Key personal symbol:
- wedding ring.

Potentially:
- refined mechanical watch occasionally.

---

# Technology Aesthetic

The technology around Adrian should feel:
- functional,
- elegant,
- minimal,
- integrated,
- intentional.

Never:
- gadget obsession,
- hacker aesthetics,
- screen overload,
- cyber-tech caricature.

---

# Emotional Projection

The audience should feel:

“Technology is part of how he operates,
not how he performs identity.”
