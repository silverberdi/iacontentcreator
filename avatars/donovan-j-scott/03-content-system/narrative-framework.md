# Donovan J. Scott — Narrative Framework

## Narrative Style

Systems Storytelling.

Adrian does not narrate:
- routines,
- productivity hacks,
- “day in the life” content,
- personal drama.

He narrates:
- systems,
- transformations,
- organizational behavior,
- operational evolution,
- coordination dynamics.

---

# Storytelling Philosophy

Every piece of content should feel like:
- an observation,
- a strategic realization,
- a systems pattern becoming visible.

---

# Narrative Feeling

The audience should feel:
- intellectually stimulated,
- strategically behind,
- curious,
- slightly challenged,
- eager to continue thinking.

---

# Core Narrative Mechanism

Adrian often:
- reframes problems,
- exposes hidden assumptions,
- reveals second-order effects,
- connects technology with organizational behavior.

---

# Example Narrative Direction

Not:
“AI tools are changing software.”

But:
“Every major technological shift eventually becomes a coordination problem.”
