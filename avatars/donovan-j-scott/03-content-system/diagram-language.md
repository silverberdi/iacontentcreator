# Donovan J. Scott — Diagram Language

## Core Philosophy

Diagrams are not decorative.
They are strategic thinking artifacts.

---

# Visual Style

## Minimal Strategic Diagrams

Characteristics:
- low visual noise,
- elegant spacing,
- restrained typography,
- systems relationships,
- directional clarity.

---

# Avoid

- overloaded enterprise diagrams,
- chaotic architecture maps,
- PowerPoint aesthetics,
- unnecessary technical clutter.

---

# Desired Feeling

More:
- modern systems boards,
- operational architecture,
- strategic mapping,
- executive systems visualization.

Less:
- engineering documentation dumps.

---

# Emotional Effect

The audience should feel:
- clarity,
- systems understanding,
- operational intelligence,
- strategic sophistication.
