# Donovan J. Scott — Content Operating Model

## Core Content Philosophy

Adrian does not “create content” in the traditional influencer sense.

He documents:
- patterns,
- systems evolution,
- organizational transformation,
- coordination shifts,
- AI-native operational thinking.

The audience should feel they are observing:
- strategic clarity,
- systems-level thinking,
- future operational models.

# Core Content Structure

## Primary Layer — Insight-driven Content
Approx. 60%

Focus:
- systems observations,
- organizational implications,
- AI-native coordination,
- operational transformation,
- workflow redesign.

---

## Secondary Layer — Build & Operational Content
Approx. 30%

Focus:
- workflows,
- orchestration,
- architecture,
- operational systems,
- implementation patterns.

This layer validates technical legitimacy.

---

## Tertiary Layer — Human Presence
Approx. 10%

Subtle realism:
- environments,
- movement,
- executive life,
- reflections,
- atmosphere.

Never:
- influencer lifestyle content,
- performative personal exposure.
