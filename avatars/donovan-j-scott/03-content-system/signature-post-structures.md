# Donovan J. Scott — Signature Post Structures

## Core Philosophy

Adrian uses recurring rhetorical structures to create:
- familiarity,
- intellectual identity,
- semantic consistency,
- strategic recognition.

The goal is not repetition.
The goal is recognizable systems thinking.

---

# Signature Structures

## 1. “The real shift is…”

Purpose:
Reveal deeper operational or organizational transformation beneath surface-level trends.

Example:
“The real shift in AI isn’t model capability.
It’s organizational coordination.”

---

## 2. “Most organizations still…”

Purpose:
Expose outdated assumptions or operational inertia.

Example:
“Most organizations still approach AI as a tooling initiative rather than an operational redesign problem.”

---

## 3. “The organizations that win…”

Purpose:
Position future-oriented organizational strategy.

Example:
“The organizations that win with AI won’t be the ones using more tools.
They’ll be the ones redesigning workflows around coordination.”

---

## 4. “X is not changing Y…”

Purpose:
Reframe simplistic narratives into systems-level consequences.

Example:
“AI is not just changing software development.
It’s changing the organizational assumptions behind software teams.”

---

# Emotional Effect

The audience should feel:
- intellectually challenged,
- strategically behind,
- curious,
- engaged,
- reflective.
