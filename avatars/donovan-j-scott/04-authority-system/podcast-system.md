# Donovan J. Scott — Podcast System

## Core Philosophy

The podcast is not:
- content volume,
- entertainment,
- creator culture.

It is:
- strategic dialogue,
- systems exploration,
- executive-level conversation.

---

# Frequency

Low-frequency.

Recommended:
1–3 episodes per month maximum.

This preserves:
- quality,
- exclusivity,
- executive gravity.

---

# Format Feeling

The podcast should feel like:
- a private strategic discussion,
- an executive systems conversation,
- a calm exploration of important ideas.

---

# Guest Types

Ideal guests:
- CTOs,
- systems thinkers,
- enterprise architects,
- AI operators,
- infrastructure leaders,
- organizational strategists.

---

# Tone

Never:
- loud,
- chaotic,
- clickbait-driven,
- creator-energy focused.

Always:
- calm,
- intelligent,
- reflective,
- systems-oriented,
- high-signal.
