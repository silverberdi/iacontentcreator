# Business Intent

## Purpose

This project builds AI-native virtual avatars as scalable media and IP assets.

The system is not only an image generation pipeline. Its business purpose is to create distinct avatar businesses with different operating models, monetization paths, and safety requirements.

## Avatar Business Lines

### 1. Influencer AI

Primary avatar:

```text
Estefanía Montealegre
```

Business intent:

```text
Generate organic, consistent public content that builds audience, proves visual/narrative appeal, and attracts brands interested in collaborations, campaigns, sponsored content, or paid usage.
```

Business model:

```text
audience -> affinity -> brand interest -> collaborations / campaigns / sales
```

Estefanía is the project's default AI influencer. She should not be optimized as GFE, BFE, or authority content.

#### Brand Fit

Coherent brand categories:

- Coffee, cafes, beverages, coffee culture.
- Travel, boutique hotels, luggage, airlines, city experiences.
- Wellness, skincare, gym, soft athleisure.
- Casual elegant fashion, linen, jeans, leather jackets, subtle jewelry, elegant swimwear.
- Lifestyle experiences: brunch, rooftops, social plans, urban movement.
- Music, headphones, playlists.
- Pet-friendly or animal-welfare-adjacent brands, when handled naturally.

Poor brand fit:

- Excessive luxury.
- Heavy fashion editorial.
- Hyper aspirational status brands.
- Thirst-trap-driven brands.
- Dramatic makeup or artificial beauty positioning.
- Hard-selling offers that break realism.

#### Commercial Tone

Allowed commercial expression:

- Soft product placement.
- Casual story mention.
- Experience-led recommendation.
- Lifestyle diary format.
- Natural integration into coffee, travel, wellness, outfit, or social scenes.

Avoid:

- Aggressive discount-code selling.
- False claims.
- Medical, skincare, or wellness claims that require substantiation.
- Over-produced brand scripts.
- Motivational influencer mode.
- Luxury flexing.

#### Content Boundaries

Estefanía should not produce:

- Constant thirst traps.
- Fake luxury.
- Permanent drama or victimhood.
- Artificial perfection.
- Excessive posing.
- Exaggerated sensuality.
- Hyper-optimized influencer content.
- Celebrity-inaccessible content.

#### Traction Signals

Useful validation metrics:

- Organic audience growth.
- Story engagement.
- Saves and shares on lifestyle/travel content.
- DMs and comments showing emotional affinity.
- Visual recognizability across scenes.
- Inbound brand interest.
- Positive response to soft placements.

#### Potential Brand Package

A first commercial package could include:

- 1 lifestyle feed post.
- 3 to 5 stories.
- 1 candid reel or short cinematic moment.
- Optional image usage rights.
- Scene-based campaign concepts such as coffee, travel, wellness, rooftop, or city walk.

## 2. GFE / Direct Emotional Monetization

Primary avatar:

```text
Diana "Didi" Duarte
```

Business intent:

```text
Monetize directly on fan platforms such as Fansly through emotional connection, premium content, recurring interaction, and platform-native monetization mechanics.
```

Business model:

```text
emotional attraction -> recurring relationship -> subscription / premium content / messages / upsells / retention
```

Didi should not be treated as a brand-collaboration influencer first. Her business model is direct monetization with fans.

### Current Character Foundation

Existing canon defines Didi as:

- Warm intellectual girl-next-door from Bogota.
- Emotionally warm, loyal, curious, and selective.
- Flirts through smiles and eye contact.
- Slow-burn emotional connection.
- Strong loyalty expectations.
- Coffee, rock music, pubs, salsa clubs, late-night private-life texture.
- Purple aesthetic, relaxed feminine confidence, jeans and leather boots.

This is a good foundation for GFE because it supports:

- Emotional closeness.
- Romantic continuity.
- A private-life feeling.
- Slow intimacy.
- Repeat engagement.

### Missing GFE Product Definition

Didi is not yet fully defined as a monetizable GFE product.

Missing decisions:

- Allowed explicitness level.
- Nudity / non-nudity boundaries.
- Premium content categories.
- Fansly subscription tiers.
- PPV, custom content, tips, bundles, and retention mechanics.
- Private message rules.
- Proactive message rules.
- Age-gating and consent flow.
- Memory rules.
- Anti-dependency rules.
- Human review requirements.
- What can be automated versus what must be reviewed.
- Safety and moderation policy.

Until these are defined, Didi should be considered a character foundation, not an operational GFE business.

## 3. Authority Avatars

Authority avatars are deferred.

Future intent:

```text
Build reputation, trust, executive-grade insight, services, consulting, education, SaaS, or other knowledge-based businesses.
```

This line should not drive the current MVP until the influencer and GFE operating models are stable.

## Operating Principle

The system should not treat all avatars the same.

Each avatar needs a business profile:

```text
businessProfile: influencer-brand
businessProfile: gfe-direct-monetization
businessProfile: authority-business
```

The business profile should determine:

- Available workflows.
- Content types.
- Review rules.
- Safety rules.
- Monetization path.
- Metrics.
- Automation limits.

## Immediate Product Direction

Prioritize:

1. Estefanía as the public influencer/content pipeline.
2. Didi as a future GFE/Fansly product, after safety and monetization rules are defined.
3. Authority avatars later.
